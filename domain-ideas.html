Naming tips

What makes a good domain name

Keep it short

Under 12 characters is ideal — easy to type on a phone and easy to say out loud.

Easy to spell

If you have to spell it out, customers will mistype it. Avoid hyphens, numbers and clever misspellings.

Say it out loud

Say it to someone who hasn’t seen it written down. If they can’t spell it back, keep looking.

Start with your own name

Your business name matches your signage and invoices — and it’s what people search for once they know you.

Local trust, global reach

.my and .com.my signal a Malaysian business; .com travels everywhere. Many businesses register both.

Check trademarks

A great name that clashes with a registered mark is a liability. Search the MyIPO database before you commit.

Is the domain name generator free?

Yes. It’s free and needs no account. We limit each visitor to a handful of generations every few minutes so it stays fast for everyone.

Are the suggested names available to register?

The AI suggests names; it doesn’t check the registry. Click “Check availability” on any idea and our system checks it live before you register.

Which extensions can I choose?

Popular worldwide (.com, .net, .org, .co, .io, .info, .biz, .me, .xyz), Malaysian (.my, .com.my, .net.my, .org.my, .asia), business and tech (.tech, .digital, .pro, .app, .dev, .ai, .cloud, .agency) and shop or creative (.shop, .store, .online, .site, .studio, .cafe) — pick up to six per run.

Is my business description stored?

It is used only to generate ideas and briefly cached so repeat requests are instant. It is not shared or used for marketing.

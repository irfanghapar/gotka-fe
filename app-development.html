What kind of projects do you take on?

Mobile apps, web applications, internal business systems and integrations between tools you already use. If you're not sure whether your idea fits, tell us about it and we'll give you an honest read.

Do you build native apps or cross-platform?

Either — we choose based on what the project needs. Cross-platform frameworks cover most apps efficiently; performance-critical or platform-specific features may call for native development instead.

How is a project priced?

As a starting point: web apps and dashboards from RM8,000, business systems and integrations from RM12,000, and mobile apps from RM15,000. These are indicative starting prices — every project is still scoped and quoted individually based on complexity, timeline and features. We'll walk through requirements with you first, then provide a clear written quote before any development begins.

Who owns the code once it's built?

You do. Full ownership of the custom code we write for you transfers on completion of payment, the same as our web design projects.

Can you take over or extend an existing app or system?

Often, yes — we'll review the existing codebase first to give you an honest assessment of what's involved before quoting the work.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing support and maintenance plans for bug fixes, updates and new features, so the same team that built your system keeps it running.

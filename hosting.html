What is cloud hosting?

Cloud hosting enhances your website's scalability and performance by running it on cloud infrastructure. You get improved reliability, and resources that grow with you.

What are the key features of these plans?

Each plan includes AI tools (an AI website builder, writing assistant and image generator), cPanel to manage your website, email and databases, SSD storage for faster load times, an SSL certificate to secure your site, automatic daily backups, and speed and security optimisations built in.

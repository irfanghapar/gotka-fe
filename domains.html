How do I register a domain name with Gotka?

Search for your name, choose the extension (.com, .my, .com.my and hundreds more), fill in your details and check out — the domain is live within minutes. Registration starts from RM10/year, transfers in are free, and every domain includes domain lock, WHOIS privacy and full ownership in your name.

What is a domain name?

A domain name is the name of your website — your web address. Every website needs one to be searchable and reachable on the web.

How does registration work?

Check availability, choose the name you want, fill in your details, then proceed to checkout and payment. Your domain is live within minutes.

Why is a domain name important?

It represents your brand identity online. A good domain name is like a physical store in the most strategic location you can find — it brings customers to you.

How can I protect my domain name?

Enable domain lock to prevent unauthorised transfers, use WHOIS privacy protection, renew your domain on time, and use a strong registrar account password and MFA.

Can I transfer my domain to another registrar?

Yes. Most domains can be transferred between registrars as long as they're unlocked and at least 60 days past registration.

Can I change my domain name later?

An existing domain can't be renamed — you'd register a new one and migrate your website and services to it.

How long is a domain name valid?

Domains are typically registered for 1 to 10 years, and you can renew them before they expire.

Who owns the domain after registration?

You (or your organisation) are the legal owner for as long as the domain is registered under your name and contact details.

Can I deregister after registration?

No — registration fees are non-refundable and a registered domain can't be deregistered. You remain the owner for the full 365 days.

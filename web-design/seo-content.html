Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Content plans are billed monthly with no lock-in — cancel, upgrade or downgrade anytime.

What does each blog post include?

Every article is keyword-mapped to a topic your customers search for, then fully optimised — H1/H2/H3 headings, a meta title and description, image alt text and a clean URL slug — and published directly to your website.

Can I order a single article without a monthly plan?

Yes. Standalone SEO-optimised articles are available from RM200 to RM350 per post (1,000 to 1,500 words), priced by word count and research complexity.

What is GEO / AI Search content tuning?

It's structuring content — clear headings, FAQs, bullet lists, schema markup — so AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews can read, summarise and cite it, on top of standard search-engine optimisation.

Does my site need to be hosted with Gotka to get a content plan?

No, a content plan can be added to any website we can publish directly to. If your site runs on a platform we can't publish to, we'll let you know before starting.

How do I sign up for a content plan?

Pick a plan and send your request directly from this page, or add it as step 5 of our website package builder if you're building a new site.

How much does monthly SEO content cost?

SEO Starter is RM900/month (2 new posts), SEO Growth RM1,800/month (4 new posts) and SEO Accelerate RM3,200/month (8 new posts with full GEO tuning); single SEO-optimised articles are also available from RM200 per post without a monthly plan.

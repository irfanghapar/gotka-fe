Quick answer

How much does a business website cost in Malaysia with Gotka? Gotka Technologies' website packages start from RM5,300 for a Starter site of up to 5 pages, RM7,800 for a Business site of up to 10 pages and RM13,800 for a Growth site of up to 20 pages. Every package includes basic SEO, mobile-responsive design, page-speed optimisation, a contact form, a WhatsApp button and SSL. Extras such as a Bahasa Malaysia or Chinese version (RM400 per page), a blog (RM500), site search, forms or GEO (from RM1,200) are added per item, and the final price is confirmed in a written quote.

    Is the estimated price the final price?

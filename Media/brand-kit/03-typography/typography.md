# Gotka Technologies — Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Bricolage Grotesque | 700, 800 | Headlines, plan names, big numbers |
| Body | Instrument Sans | 400, 500, 600 | Paragraphs, UI labels, buttons |
| Data | IBM Plex Mono | 400, 500, 600 | Prices, specs, eyebrows, tickers |

All three are free Google Fonts.

Embed on the web:
```html
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Bricolage+Grotesque:opsz,wght@12..96,700;12..96,800&family=Instrument+Sans:wght@400;500;600&family=IBM+Plex+Mono:wght@400;500;600&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```

Rules of thumb: eyebrows/labels are IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, letter-spaced (~0.14–0.22em).
Headlines are Bricolage 700/800 with tight (-1%) letter-spacing. Never letter-space body text.

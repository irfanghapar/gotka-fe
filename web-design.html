What does Gotka's web design service include?

Gotka Technologies designs, builds and maintains business websites in Malaysia: design and development, domain, business email and DNS, hosting on Gotka's own LiteSpeed cloud, and ongoing maintenance. Existing sites are migrated for free with no downtime, and you can edit your site yourself through cPanel and the AI website builder.

How much does a website cost with Gotka?

Web design packages start from RM5,300 for a Starter site of up to 5 pages, RM7,800 for a Business site of up to 10 pages and RM13,800 for a Growth site of up to 20 pages — indicative prices confirmed in a written quote. Every package includes basic SEO, mobile-responsive design, page-speed optimisation, a contact form, a WhatsApp button and SSL.

Can I see examples of your work?

Absolutely — send us a message and we'll share our portfolio with you.

I already have a website. Can you take it over?

Yes. We migrate sites to Gotka hosting for free — with no downtime or data loss — and can redesign or maintain them from there.

Can I edit the website myself after launch?

Yes. Sites on our hosting include the AI website builder and cPanel access, so quick edits are easy — and we're here for anything bigger.

Do you handle the domain and email too?

Yes — domain registration or transfer, business email accounts and DNS are handled by the same team and managed in one client area. Hosting and the domain are quoted together with your package.

Is the estimated price the final price?

No — it is an indicative figure to help you plan. After you send your request we review what you have chosen, ask a few questions if needed, and confirm the price in a written quote before any work starts. The final figure is usually close to the estimate unless the scope changes.

What is not included?

Website copy, photos and videos are supplied by you unless we agree otherwise. Hosting and the domain are quoted separately unless you add them to your request. Anything not listed on this page is scoped and priced in your quote.

Can I add or remove options later?

Yes. The package is a starting point, not a contract — options can be added or removed before we start, and most can be added after launch too. Additional pages, a blog or a new language version are common later additions.

Do I own the finished website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, the site runs in your own Gotka client account, and you can edit it yourself through cPanel and the AI website builder — or ask us to.

Can I cancel or change the maintenance or content plan later?

Yes. Both are billed monthly with no lock-in — upgrade, downgrade or cancel either one anytime by contacting us. Work already in progress for that month is completed first.

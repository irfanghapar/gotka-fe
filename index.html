How much does a website cost with Gotka?

Web design packages start from RM5,300 for a Starter site of up to 5 pages, RM7,800 for a Business site of up to 10 pages and RM13,800 for a Growth site of up to 20 pages — indicative prices confirmed in a written quote before any work starts.

Can I move my existing website to Gotka?

Yes, and migration is free on every plan. Our engineers move your files, databases and email over for you, with no downtime and no data loss.

Which hosting plan is right for me?

As a rule of thumb: Go AI Lite is ideal for small startups, Go AI Plus suits growing businesses, Go AI Pro is for expanding enterprises needing more resources, and Go AI Max gives top-tier performance and reliability.

What is the uptime guarantee?

Gotka Technologies guarantees 99.9% uptime on all AI-Powered cPanel Web Hosting plans, so your website stays accessible to visitors with minimal downtime.

Is a free domain name included?

Yes — all AI-Powered cPanel Web Hosting plans except Go AI Lite include a free domain when you subscribe to a 12-month or longer plan.
